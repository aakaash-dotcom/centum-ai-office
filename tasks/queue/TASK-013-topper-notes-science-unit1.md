## Why this task
Topper Notes are what the parent is paying for and what the free shelf must never show. They are not "shorter complete notes" — they are what the examiner ticks: verbatim definitions, formulae, mark splits, model answers, and the traps that cost marks.

## Steps
1. Open with a half-page "what the examiner wants in this unit" — mark distribution per topic, based on the gated PYQ frequency (count how often each topic appeared 2022–2026).
2. Verbatim-writable definitions for every named concept/term in the unit.
3. One formula box: formula, meaning of each symbol, SI unit, one worked example.
4. Mark-splitting for at least 3 long answers, written as the teacher's tick sequence.
5. Five model answers written full length, in exam-friendly language.
6. A traps list: the 6–10 most common mistakes in this unit, each with its correction beside it.
7. Layout per house style: A5, ruled/structured, brand line in the footer, page numbers, readable at phone zoom.
8. **Folder note:** the tree in `OFFICE.md` §5.10 has no `Notes/` folder yet. Create `StudyHub/TN/10th/Science/Notes/` for notes and say so in your log, so the Manager can update `OFFICE.md` §5.10 and `ledgers/drive_map.json`. Never file notes into `OneWord/`, `QBank/`, `SlowLearners/`, or `PYQ/`.
